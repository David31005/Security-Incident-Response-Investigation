5. RECOMMENDED RESPONSE

  authorized.
- Review authentication and privilege audit logs.
- Preserve relevant logs and evidence.
- Check for additional suspicious account activity.
- If unauthorized access is confirmed, follow
  approved account containment procedures.
- Reset credentials and revoke sessions if warranted.
- Document investigation and recovery actions.
